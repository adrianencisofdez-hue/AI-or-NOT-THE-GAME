# Image Dataset Specification

## 1. Folder structure

The dataset folder must follow this structure:

- `assets/images/ai/easy/`
- `assets/images/ai/medium/`
- `assets/images/ai/hard/`
- `assets/images/ai/impossible/`
- `assets/images/real/easy/`
- `assets/images/real/medium/`
- `assets/images/real/hard/`
- `assets/images/real/impossible/`

Each folder contains images for that category and difficulty only.

## 2. Naming convention

All image filenames must use this pattern:

`<type>_<difficulty>_<three_digit_id>.webp`

Where:
- `<type>` is `ai` or `real`
- `<difficulty>` is `easy`, `medium`, `hard`, or `impossible`
- `<three_digit_id>` is a zero-padded numeric identifier, for example `001`, `012`, `100`

Example:
- `ai_easy_001.webp`
- `real_impossible_023.webp`

## 3. Difficulty levels

### Easy
- Clear visual distinction between AI and real concepts.
- Simple subjects and obvious cues.
- Minimal ambiguity.

### Medium
- Moderately subtle presentation.
- Some visual complexity and nuance.
- Still reasonably identifiable with attention.

### Hard
- High ambiguity between AI and real imagery.
- More sophisticated imagery, lighting, or composition.
- Requires careful observation.

### Impossible
- Extremely subtle or deceptive imagery.
- Near-indistinguishable AI and real quality.
- Designed for advanced evaluation, not casual guessing.

## 4. Rules for AI images

- Use synthetic imagery generated by AI tools or models.
- Maintain consistent quality and realism appropriate to the selected difficulty.
- Avoid obvious artifacts only in easier levels; higher levels may include sophisticated subtlety.
- Do not use existing copyrighted photography as the base without proper authorization.
- Ensure AI images match the folder difficulty and naming conventions.

## 5. Rules for REAL images

- Use authentic photographs or real-world captures.
- Images may be sourced from public domain, properly licensed collections, or original photography.
- Avoid altered or heavily edited real images that become synthetic-looking unless the difficulty level justifies it.
- Ensure real images are genuine and not misrepresented AI creations.

## 6. Minimum image quality requirements

- File format: `.webp`
- Resolution: at least 1024x1024 pixels or equivalent aspect ratio quality.
- Image must be sharp and readable.
- No extreme compression artifacts.
- Visual content must be clear enough for user evaluation.

## 7. Forbidden images

Do not include images with:

- Visible watermarks, logos, or branded overlays.
- Copyrighted content used without permission.
- Low resolution or heavy compression artifacts.
- Screenshots of user interfaces, websites, or device screens.
- Obvious text-only memes, collages, or graphics that are not representative visual images.
- Direct reproductions of copyrighted artwork, movie stills, or celebrity images without rights.

## 8. Future expansion rules

- New difficulty categories may be added only if the folder structure and naming conventions are updated consistently.
- Additional image types beyond `ai` and `real` require a formal spec update.
- Keep the folder hierarchy stable: `assets/images/<type>/<difficulty>/`.
- Preserve dataset consistency by adding images with valid filenames and matching difficulty semantics.
- Maintain backward compatibility with the existing catalog format.
